Help for gambling

Only you can decide whether you are a compulsive gambler.

Casinos, sports betting, video poker, the track, an app on your phone — the form does not matter. What matters is whether you can stop. Twenty questions have helped a lot of people in this area answer that honestly for the first time.

There are no dues or fees. You do not have to give your name. Anyone who wants to stop gambling is welcome at any meeting on this map.

No meeting near you?

Start one. It takes two people and a room.

Half of Area 7 is more than an hour from the nearest meeting. If that is where you live, the area will help you open one: starter literature, a format to read from, a contact at the closest group, and a listing on the map the day you hold your first meeting.

START A MEETING IN MY TOWN
1
Tell the area where you are. A trusted servant calls you back within a week.
2
Find a room — a church hall, a library, a hospital meeting room. Most are free to recovery groups.
3
Area sends literature and a meeting format, and lists you on this map and with the International Service Office.
4
Hold the meeting, whether one person comes or twelve. Keep holding it.
