Routes a visitor to their local intergroup, or tells them honestly that there isn't one.

```jsx
<IntergroupCard
  name="Baton Rouge Area"
  description="Baton Rouge, Hammond and the Northshore. Nine weekly meetings and a 24-hour hotline."
  linkLabel="batonrougega.org" href="https://batonrougega.org"
/>
<IntergroupCard
  status="none" name="Ask the area"
  description="Jackson, the Gulf Coast and Mobile are served by their groups directly."
  linkLabel="Reach the area secretary" href="/contact"
/>
```

**Only Baton Rouge and Shreveport are confirmed.** Everything else is
`status="none"` until an intergroup actually forms — do not imply coverage
that does not exist.
