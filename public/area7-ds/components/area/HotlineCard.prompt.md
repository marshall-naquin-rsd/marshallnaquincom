Someone in trouble should find a number without scrolling. Put these on the home page and the contact page.

```jsx
<HotlineCard label="Baton Rouge & Northshore" number="888-502-5610"
  hours="Answered 24 hours a day, seven days a week." />
<HotlineCard label="Area 7 hotline" pending
  hours="Area 7 has not yet established a single number." />
```

Use `pending` rather than omitting the card — a visible gap invites the area
to fill it. Never state hours a volunteer cannot cover.
