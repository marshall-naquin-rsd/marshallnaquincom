Procedures a group has to carry out — starting a meeting, seating a GSR, requesting literature.

```jsx
<StepList steps={[
  'Tell the area where you are. A trusted servant calls you back within a week.',
  'Find a room — a church hall, a library, a hospital meeting room.',
  'Area sends literature and a meeting format, and lists you on the map.',
  'Hold the meeting, whether one person comes or twelve. Keep holding it.',
]} />
```

Only legible on `--heron-deep`; the step tiles are a fixed #25353F.
